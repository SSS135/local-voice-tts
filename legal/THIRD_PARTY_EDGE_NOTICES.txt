Reader Next 3.4.4 Kotlin Edge client (GPLv3 application) and edge-tts 7.3.1

Pinned upstream commit: 360fa8e1de354e1dda74c09bd695887d6a44df74

Adapted source files: EdgeGec.java, EdgeProtocol.java, EdgeBackend.java.
Changes: Java synchronous SpeechBackend interface; direct cancellation hook;
bounded in-memory MP3/MediaCodec mono PCM decoding; Unicode-safe text turns;
no initialization/catalog request, retry after authentication denial, or fallback.
Each online turn buffers its complete MP3 response in memory (maximum 2 MiB)
then validates complete MP3 frames and stages all MediaCodec decoded PCM
before the first client PCM write. Staged PCM is capped at 180 seconds mono
24 kHz float audio (4,320,000 samples, about 17 MiB) per turn. This is not a
live network-to-decoder stream; first PCM begins after full turn validation.
There is a 45-second network attempt deadline and a 20-second codec attempt
deadline. Recoverable failures retry the same uncommitted chunk indefinitely
with cancellation-aware network waits and exponential jittered backoff capped
at 15 seconds; supplied Retry-After delays are also honored. Authentication,
invalid voice/configuration, and unsupported format errors fail immediately.
No audio from a failed attempt reaches the client; previously committed chunks
are not retried. User cancellation closes the socket and stops waiting/output.
This is chunk-level recovery and does not claim exact-word playback resume.
