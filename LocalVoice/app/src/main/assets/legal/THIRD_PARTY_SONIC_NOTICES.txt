Local modifications, 2026-10-18: changed Java package to org.localvoice.tts.backend;
replaced the single AndroidX Assertions.checkState dependency with a local
IllegalStateException check; widened the two AMDF pitch-comparison products to
long to prevent integer overflow at high sample rates (96 kHz tone regression).
No complete Media3 library or dependency is added.

PcmTimeStretch is a mono float-PCM adapter with bounded 2048-sample input/output
blocks. Speed 1 bypasses Sonic without quantization. Other speeds use Sonic's
16-bit core with pitch fixed at 1 and identical input/output sample rates.
Its per-utterance lifecycle checks cancellation around processing and output,
discards pending audio after cancellation or sink rejection, and flushes once.
